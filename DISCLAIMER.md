# Research and safety disclaimer

**CSRP Paper Skills is a research prototype.**

It is designed to study architectures for scientific evidence organization and cross-paper reasoning. It is not validated for clinical use, diagnosis, triage, treatment selection, or individual suicide-risk prediction.

The real-paper records currently included in the repository are structured research artifacts curated from public sources. They are not yet expert-validated gold annotations and should not be treated as a systematic review, clinical guideline, or authoritative evidence synthesis.

Any individual-facing application would require substantially stronger validation, governance, privacy controls, human oversight, and domain-specific safety review.
