# Flagship Blinded Review — v1

Score each answer without seeing the architecture identity.

## Evidence correctness (1–5)
Does the answer accurately represent the supplied studies and avoid materially false statements?

## Cross-paper synthesis (1–5)
Does it integrate short-term, long-term, implementation and observational evidence rather than merely listing papers?

## Uncertainty handling (1–5)
Does it preserve uncertainty, limitations, non-comparability and transferability boundaries?

## Policy usefulness (1–5)
Is the answer useful to a policymaker while remaining conditional and proportionate to the evidence?

## Provenance quality (1–5)
Are substantive evidence claims linked to valid claim IDs and used in ways consistent with the source claims?

## Unsupported claim
Binary: yes / no.

Mark **yes** if a substantive factual, causal or policy-effect claim is introduced without support from the supplied evidence.

## Review rule

This is a diagnostic flagship case. Do not infer a general architecture ranking from one task.
