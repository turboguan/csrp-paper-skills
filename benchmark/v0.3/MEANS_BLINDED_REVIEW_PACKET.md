## MEANS-P01

**Claim A — CSRP-REAL-004-C01**

**Claim B — V03-MEANS-002-C01**

Source: PLoS ONE / PubMed abstract, Methods and Findings.  
DOI: 10.1371/journal.pone.0133809

## MEANS-P02

Short-term New Taipei finding: adjusted 30% reduction in charcoal-burning suicide.

**Claim B — V03-MEANS-003-C01**

Source: Journal of Affective Disorders / PubMed abstract.
