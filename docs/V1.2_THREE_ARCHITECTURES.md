# v1.2 — Three-Architecture Comparison Reset

## Change

The active three-architecture experiment is now:

1. **Functional Virtual Institute**
2. **Minimal Virtual CSRP**
3. **Shared-Knowledge Umbrella**

The previous **Paper-Agent Committee** is no longer Architecture 2 in the active comparison.

It remains in the repository for historical reference and possible later ablation, but it is removed from the primary pre-pilot.

## Why change Architecture 2?

The committee architecture creates one agent per paper plus a chair. That is useful as a multi-agent baseline, but it is not the most plausible design for an AI-native virtual research center.

The new question is more direct:

> **How much specialization does a virtual research center actually need?**

The three architectures now form a cleaner organizational spectrum.

## Architecture 1 — Functional Virtual Institute

```text
                    Facilitator
                         │
      ┌──────────┬───────┼──────────┬──────────┐
      ▼          ▼       ▼          ▼          ▼
Perspective   Predict  Strategy   External   Decision
      └──────────┴───────┴──────────┴──────────┘
                         │
                 Shared Evidence
```

**Logic:** high specialization, horizontal role division.

**Typical live LLM calls:** 5.

It asks whether explicit specialist roles improve reasoning enough to justify their coordination cost.

## Architecture 2 — Minimal Virtual CSRP

```text
Research Question
      ↓
Research Coordinator
      ↓
Evidence & Data
      ↓
Analysis & Methods  ← activated only when needed
      ↓
Synthesis & Decision
      ↓
Quality & Safety Gate
```

Shared infrastructure:

```text
Paper Skills
Evidence Graph
Provenance Registry
Datasets / analysis artifacts
Prior research outputs
```

**Logic:** moderate specialization, sequential research-process division.

For evidence-synthesis tasks, three agents are normally active:

```text
Coordinator → Evidence & Data → Synthesis & Decision
```

The Analysis & Methods Agent is activated only when the task requires actual statistical or computational analysis.

**Typical evidence-synthesis LLM calls:** 3.

## Architecture 3 — Shared-Knowledge Umbrella

```text
Shared Paper Skills + Evidence Graph
                ↓
        Institutional Agent
                ↓
       Evidence / Safety checks
                ↓
            Answer
```

**Logic:** low specialization, centralized shared-knowledge reasoning.

**Typical live LLM calls:** 1.

It tests whether a single institutional reasoning context can integrate the evidence without explicit internal organizational specialization.

## The three-way contrast

The experiment is no longer:

```text
functional roles
vs
paper committee
vs
shared knowledge
```

It is now:

```text
HIGH specialization
Functional Virtual Institute
        ↓
MODERATE specialization
Minimal Virtual CSRP
        ↓
LOW specialization
Shared-Knowledge Umbrella
```

This gives a more coherent management-science question:

> **What level and form of organizational specialization is optimal for an AI research center, and how does that depend on the task?**

## Experimental controls

Across all three architectures keep constant:

- corpus;
- question;
- retriever;
- EvidencePacket;
- evidence budget;
- base LLM/model;
- decoding settings;
- total token ceiling;
- safety boundary.

Coordination structure and agent specialization are the treatment.

## Paper-Agent Committee

The Paper-Agent Committee is **not deleted**.

It remains useful for:

- later multi-agent baseline comparison;
- scaling/coordination-cost ablation;
- studying one-paper-one-agent designs;
- direct comparison with Paper2Agent-style organizational interpretations.

It is simply no longer one of the three primary architectures.
